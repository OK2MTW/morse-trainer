Method – inspiration

The Koch method (Ludwig Koch, 1936) and the character order of LCWO.net (Fabian Kurz, DJ5CW); Farnsworth timing; instant character recognition (ICR) training after morsecode.world and CWops CW Academy; findings on differences between learners (Long Island CW Club). We do not take over texts and content of these projects – only principles.

Vocabulary content

Abbreviations and prefixes after the exam questions of the Czech Telecommunication Office CTU (06/2025); the selection of Q codes and their meanings after the Q-kodex of the Czech Radio Club. Texts are in our own words.
